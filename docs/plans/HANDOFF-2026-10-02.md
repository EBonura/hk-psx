## Performance pass (2026-10-03)

Goal: every gameplay frame within 2 vblanks (boot, loads and CD windows excluded), boss fights
first. Measured headless on PSoXide 020163fe over 52 routes: the 50 builder routes plus two
Greenpath tapes (f01-moss, f17-charger). A frame is over budget when its flip comes more than two
vblanks after the previous one, counting gameplay ticks only.

Landed on main (on top of fdfe701):

- af20290: scenery draws are read in place from the scene bank, not copied into a 48-byte array
  per view, and what a view bind derives for each draw is kept per scene pool draw. RAM: free
  below the module pool went from 1,152 to 17,940 bytes.
- 3e082e1: the scene's draw pool is derived a couple of draws at a time in the idle wait before a
  flip, so a view bind mid-fight no longer derives every draw in the frame that needs it.
- 90f959b: terrain edges compare word by word (`hk_sim::same_edge`/`empty_edge`), not through a
  16-byte `memcmp` call.
- d0998a5: the ambience clip check validates headers by stride and hashes in registers (it ran
  byte by byte from inside the drive pump during play).
- 5974ac3: particle scans stop at the highest occupied slot; `lerp` takes a 32-bit path.
- 078c932: a visual fix. The hero light and the scenery's occlusion trimming share the frame's
  optional packets; trimming runs only after GPU-bound frames, so the light was dropped on about
  a thousand frames over the routes (`HK_GLOW_SKIPPED`) and came and went with the frame rate.
  Trimming now leaves the light eight packets.

Gates: validate 54/54 with no faults, host suite 795 OK, hk-sim tests OK, budget doc test OK.
Pixels: lockstep builds (render only on every sixth consumed tick) without the hero vignette,
fdfe701 plus the light fix against this build; frames dumped at the same input poll are
pixel-identical (cornifer-map, mound-spell, town-shade, gruz-fight stations). With the vignette
on, frames differ by one 5-bit step in scattered pixels: the vignette words are refreshed a
sixteenth at a time per rendered frame, so they follow the frame rate (pre-existing).

What is left, and why:

- GPU-bound rooms (Greenpath, Crossroads_04 for Gruz Mother, mound-spell, crossroads-gate). With
  GPU cost removed in the emulator the 1,982 baseline drops to 880, so overdraw is the biggest
  lever. A dumped secret-f08a frame writes 8.1 screens of pixels and 78% are hidden by later
  opaque pixels; 62% could be trimmed at 16 px cells if the opacity of several draws is unioned.
  That is the approved per-draw sub-tile opacity mask work, ported onto branch `perf/hk-cells`
  (8 px certified cells unioned into 16 px tiles, from wip/hk-camera-cell-coverage). It needs a
  certificate recook (constant edits in host/opaque_tiles.py and host/scene_certificates.py) and
  has not been built or measured.
- King's Pass particle bursts (kings-climb, ctrl-climb, kings-return, cheat-dream, kings-death):
  nearly every slow frame follows a burst of about 100 debris particles; with particle code free
  in the emulator kings-climb drops from 185 to 9. The remaining cost is the per-particle tick
  (about 330 instructions plus a 64-bit square root and a 64-bit division above the speed
  limit) and the per-particle draw.
- secret-c03/c37: enemy AI per tick (walker queries, actor steps, line of sight) plus scenery.
- Frame pacing is bistable: occlusion trimming switches on after a GPU-bound frame, which can
  lock a route into alternating 2/3-vblank frames. Routes move by up to about 40 frames between
  builds that do not change their code, so compare totals, not single routes.

Tools (not in this repo): the hk-perf work dir has a Rust measurement driver (`hkperf`: sweeps,
budget tables, lockstep gate, profiles, overdraw cells), a copy of the emulator with limit oracles
(01c5a08 plus a GPU cost scale), and a pixel-owner emulator that logs every pixel write.

## Playtest fixes (2026-10-03, merged to main 1893d92)

Manny played the library disc (8935fe58) and recorded King's Pass; the tape is now the validate
route `kp-playtest`.

- Scenery going dark or vanishing while walking. Not the performance pass: the same frames are
  wrong on the pre-pass build (fdfe701). In King's Pass the Knight stays in view 1 while the
  camera draws view 2 or 12, and the drawn view switches at x 48. The hero vignette's words are
  indexed by the bound view's draws and refreshed a sixteenth a frame, and a bind kept the old
  view's words, so scenery took other draws' colour and darkness (black far from the Knight) and
  came back piece by piece. `render::init` now remakes every word on the next `set_vignette`, and
  a draw's colour change (a fade) remakes its word from its kept factor. `HK_VIGNETTE_STALE`
  counts words found not to match their draw (one checked a frame) and validate requires 0 on
  every route; a build without the init reset ends kp-playtest at 1,287, kings-climb at 1,479.
- Stalactites: a nail hit used to remove one outright. As `StalactiteControl.OnTriggerEnter2D`
  does, a hit now makes it harmless; an upward slash breaks it, a side slash bats it 45 degrees
  below the horizontal and a downward one straight down, at hitVelocity 50 without gravity, until
  it embeds. Its break effects (hitUpEffectPrefabs, 10 to 12 rocks from hitUpRockPrefabs), the
  nail strike prefab and its sounds are not cooked (that needs host/props.py).
- Particles. Door Break Dust was a bright dithered block, and every break particle (dust and
  rocks) drew at its texel's own colour: `render::resident_quad` forced tint 128, so the authored
  colours (rocks 69/101, dust 88,93,106) never reached the GPU. Break effects now draw through
  `resident_quad_tinted` in their colours, and the faint styles (start or curve alpha under 255:
  Break Dust, texture 371, in every scene) draw as soft added light: palette copies with the
  semi-transparency bit in free slots (368,236..239), AddQuarter at colour times opacity
  (`B + a*F/4`). Guest-side only, no re-cook. On Manny's tape the five door-break windows have 81
  frames over two vblanks against 79, the whole tape 89 against 87 (worst 4 in both), within the
  bistable spread. RAM: 4,104 bytes, mostly code across an alignment step; 9,736 free below the
  pool. Comparison frames: `approval/particles-doors-og-old-new.png` in the hk-bugs work dir.
- Open: grass blades. The bits Manny saw as blue-white clumps are fern-shaped speckled sprites;
  which effect or texture draws them is not identified, and the blade texture's alpha is opaque,
  so there is nothing to blend guest-side. It likely needs a re-cook, so it waits for the Rust
  Unity port (props.py and the image ops). The frame dips on his tape are the known particle
  bursts after each door or pole break (85 of 1,073 gameplay frames over two vblanks, 84 before
  the performance pass).
- Instruction-cache layout. The vignette fix alone measured neutral (gruz-fight 193 frames over
  two vblanks against 202, boss-death 48.5 fps against 48.2). With the stalactite commit, gruz-fight
  went to 321 with the same gameplay state: over its fight the I-cache refill stalls rose from
  241.6M to 252.1M cycles. The code shift moved scenery_range against its leaves
  (alpha_scissor_cache map_cached_into, occlusion_scratch dynamic_hidden, geo_render draw_art).
  Over all routes that build had 1,877 frames over two vblanks against 1,700. Fixed by
  game/build.rs, which hands ld.lld `game/hot-text-order.txt` (23 hot functions: the renderer's
  leaves, particles, coins, the input checkpoint) as a symbol ordering file, in an order searched
  from per-instruction counts of kings-climb and gruz-fight; hk-build checks the order after each
  link. gruz-fight's refill stalls are 239.0M, the 52-route mean frame rate 34.835 against the
  library build's 34.867. The over-two-vblank totals (1,773 against 1,700, gruz-fight 247) move
  by tens of frames between layouts with the same stalls (frame pacing is bistable, see above), so
  they are not a layout measure. The names are mangled: a change of the crate hash (features,
  dependencies) makes the ordinary build fail and print the list under the new hash.
  psoxide-pgo's `+order` would replace the hand list; game/build.rs already honours its
  PSOXIDE_LINK_ORDER hook.
